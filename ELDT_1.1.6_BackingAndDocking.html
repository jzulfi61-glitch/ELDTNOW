Module 6 · Unit 1.1.6

Backing and Docking

The highest-risk maneuver — done right, every time

What You'll Learn

  • Explain why backing is the most dangerous maneuver a professional driver performs
  • Apply the GOAL procedure and general backing safety rules every time
  • Use the four steering techniques: jacking, chasing, following, and push-pull
  • Distinguish between sight side and blind side backing and the risks of each
  • Describe five backing maneuvers: straight line, alley dock, jackknife, and offset
  • Apply extra precautions required at loading docks and in parking lots
