Module 16 · Unit 1.3.2

Skid Control/Recovery, Jackknifing, and Other Emergencies

Know what causes a skid — and exactly how to recover.

What You'll Learn

  • Identify the three causes of skids: overaccelerating, overbraking, and oversteering
  • Describe the four types of tractor-trailer skids and the four-step recovery procedure
  • Explain the rules for maintaining control on slippery surfaces
  • Describe evasive steering techniques for oncoming, stopped, and merging vehicles
  • Differentiate between controlled and stab braking; explain how ABS changes the approach
  • Explain the correct response to brake failure, tire blowouts, hydroplaning, and rollovers
