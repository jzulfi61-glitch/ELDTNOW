Module 15 · Unit 1.3.1

Hazard Perception

Spot hazards early — before they become emergencies.

What You'll Learn

  • Identify road hazards: drop-offs, work zones, debris, off-ramps, and on-ramps
  • Identify user hazards: intersections, parked vehicles, pedestrians, disabled vehicles, distracted/impaired/fatigued drivers, accident scenes, and railroad crossings
  • Apply the visual search technique: 12 to 15 seconds ahead, mirrors every 4 seconds
  • Explain why steering is generally preferable to stopping in an emergency
  • Identify the two primary escape routes in an evasive maneuver
