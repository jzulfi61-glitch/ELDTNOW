Maintenance

Your vehicle stays safe, efficient, and legal — when you do your part.

What You'll Learn

  • List the three types of vehicle maintenance: routine servicing, scheduled PM, and unscheduled
  • Identify the five key regulations in Part 396 of the FMCSRs
  • Describe your four driver responsibilities: detect, inspect, verify, and report
  • Recognize the full cost of poor maintenance: breakdowns, accidents, violations, and fuel waste
