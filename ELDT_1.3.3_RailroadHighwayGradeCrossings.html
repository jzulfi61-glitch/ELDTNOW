Railroad-Highway Grade Crossings

Know the rules, know the signs, and know what to do in an emergency.

What You'll Learn

  • Explain when stopping at railroad crossings is required under §392.10 and the required stop zone
  • State the six railroad crossing violations that result in CDL disqualification
  • Identify passive signs and active signals at railroad crossings
  • Describe the correct procedure for approaching and crossing railroad tracks
  • Explain the emergency response if your vehicle becomes stuck on the tracks
