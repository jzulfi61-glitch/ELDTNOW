Module 30 · Unit 1.5.10

Medical Requirements

Stay qualified, stay certified, stay on the road.

What You'll Learn

  • List the general qualifications: age 21+, English proficiency, one valid CDL, 12-month violation list provided
  • Explain the medical exam process: NRCME-certified examiner, at least every 24 months, certificate maintained 3 years
  • State key physical standards: vision 20/40 each eye, hearing whisper at 5 feet, no epilepsy, no insulin-dependent diabetes without waiver
  • Identify disqualifying offenses including DUI, drug transport, leaving an accident scene, and OOS order violations
