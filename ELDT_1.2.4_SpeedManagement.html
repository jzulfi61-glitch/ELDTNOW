Speed Management

Know your stopping distance. Choose the right speed for every condition.

What You'll Learn

  • Describe the three components of stopping distance and the role of brake lag
  • State that a loaded CMV at 55 mph needs approximately 450 feet to stop in ideal conditions
  • Manage speed correctly through curves and on grades
  • Adjust speed appropriately for poor road conditions, weather, and traffic
  • Explain the safety and legal consequences of speeding
  • State when cruise control may and may not be used
