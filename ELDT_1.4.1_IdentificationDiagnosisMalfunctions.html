Module 18 · Unit 1.4.1

Identification and Diagnosis of Malfunctions

Understand your vehicle's systems — and recognize when something is wrong.

What You'll Learn

  • Describe the function of the frame, suspension, and axle systems
  • Explain the engine and its supporting systems: fuel, air intake, exhaust, lubrication, and cooling
  • Describe the electrical system's four circuits and the drive train components
  • Explain the brake system including service, emergency, parking brakes, and ABS
  • Describe tire and wheel requirements and the steering system
  • Use sight, sound, smell, and feel to identify potential vehicle malfunctions
