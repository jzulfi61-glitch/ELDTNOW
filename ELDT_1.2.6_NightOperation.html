Night Operation

Prepare your truck, read the road, and know your limits after dark.

What You'll Learn

  • Describe the pre-trip inspection items critical for night driving
  • Use low and high beams correctly, including proper dimming distances
  • Identify road hazards specific to nighttime driving
  • Explain how darkness affects your vision and how to manage headlight glare
  • Recognize the warning signs of fatigue and apply countermeasures
